## Information We Collect

When you use our Apps, we may collect the following types of information:

### Information You Provide to Us
We collect information you directly provide, such as your name, email address, and any other information you choose to provide when creating an account or using our services.

### Usage and Device Information
We automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with the Apps. This may include:
- Device model
- Operating system version
- Unique device identifiers
- IP address
- App usage data (e.g., app launches, features used)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- To provide, maintain, and improve our Apps and their features
- To authenticate users and manage user accounts
- To analyze app performance and usage trends to understand how our users interact with the Apps and guide our product decisions
- To ensure the security and stability of the Apps, including debugging and crash reporting

## Third-Party Services

Some of our Apps use services provided by third-party companies. These services may collect information about you and your use of the Apps.

### Firebase
We use Firebase, a platform provided by Google, for backend services including:
- Database management
- User authentication

Firebase may collect data as described in their privacy policy. For specific details on what data Firebase collects, please see the [official Firebase documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
