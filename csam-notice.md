# DARMed Child Safety Standards

**Last Updated:** July 21, 2026

## Overview

DARMed is a professional medical network for healthcare professionals across the UAE. We are committed to maintaining a safe, secure, and respectful platform for all users. We have a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

---

## 1. Published Standards Against CSAE

DARMed strictly prohibits:

- Creation, uploading, or distribution of any content that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, including photos, videos, or computer-generated imagery involving minors engaged in sexually explicit conduct
- Grooming behavior or any inappropriate interaction targeting a minor
- Sextortion, trafficking, or any form of sexual exploitation of children
- Sharing or requesting content that facilitates child exploitation

**Consequences:** Users who violate this policy will have their accounts permanently terminated and may be reported to relevant law enforcement authorities.

---

## 2. Addressing CSAM

We take immediate and appropriate action to address CSAM:

- Remove CSAM promptly upon obtaining actual knowledge of it in our app
- Report confirmed CSAM cases to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or relevant regional authorities as required by law
- Cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations
- Maintain records of reports and actions taken for compliance purposes

---

## 3. In-App User Feedback Mechanism

Users can report concerns, inappropriate content, or policy violations directly within the DARMed app through:

- Support tab in the app.
- All reports are reviewed promptly by our moderation team

Users do not need to leave the app to submit a report.

---

## 4. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

DARMed complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including:

- UAE Federal Laws on child protection
- International child safety standards and best practices
- Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy requirements

---
